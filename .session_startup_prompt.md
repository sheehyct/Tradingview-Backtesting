**What the sector look found, in plain words** (a LEVEL is a period's opening price; a CRACK is
the day a sector fund stops being above all of its levels; DEEP means it lost three or more):

1. Most cracks are nothing: about half heal after one level, usually within three days. Each
   further level is less likely to go than the last (47%, 37%, 28%).
2. The strongest read is how tightly the levels are stacked under price. Third level about
   three daily swings away: 31% of cracks go deep. Thirteen swings away: 10%.
3. A subsector fund that has STAYED out of agreement for one to four weeks about doubles the
   chance of a deep sector crack in the next ten days (26 to 36% against 13 to 19%). The day it
   first cracks tells you nothing.
4. The count of big names already cracked looks like a tell and is mostly point 2 seen from
   inside. It adds a little only when the cushion is already thin.
5. High-beta followers (beta 1.5 or more against the sector, not a top weight) are a size tool:
   they amplify about as much up as down. Daily closes cannot see the trade inside the day.
6. Periods that share one opening price are ONE level. Counting them separately gave the
   opposite answer. The owner agreed the one-level rule "for now".

Everything in `analysis/sector/` is EXPLORATORY: no prereg, no tests, no audit, today's holdings
(hindsight), clustered dates. Nothing there is a rule.
