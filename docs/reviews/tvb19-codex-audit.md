## 2. Verbatim audit

### Verdict

NEEDS-CHANGES. The central P/L accounting and the clock-census headline survive independent
reconstruction, the warm-key optimization is valid, and the overfit/no-official-number language is
disciplined. The range nevertheless misses its own metric contract, and the deep-harvest generator
does not reproducibly establish the committed 33/33 inventory or its claimed data floors. These are
artifact-quality defects, not a reason to reject the clock result or promote a different sweep cell.

### Findings

#### 1. MEDIUM -- Sweep "median" fields are not medians, and the roster rollup omits preregistered metrics

The preregistration says the listed closed-trade and episode metrics are produced per cell/symbol and
then at roster rollup, including median trade P/L, win rate, and give-back median
(`docs/experiments/tvb19_tier_a_prereg.md:112`). The runner's `_pct` instead selects one observed
element at `round(q * (n - 1))` (`analysis/paper/sweep_tier_a.py:130`), and uses that helper for every
field labeled `med` (`analysis/paper/sweep_tier_a.py:218`, `analysis/paper/sweep_tier_a.py:231`,
`analysis/paper/sweep_tier_a.py:234`). For an even sample this is not the sample median, which is the
mean of the middle pair.

The committed artifact proves the discrepancy without needing event logs. Cell 215 / MRVL has two
closed trades totaling 41.4988pp but stores `med_pnl_pct=10.9755`; the actual two-observation median
is 20.7494pp. Its two-episode MFE and give-back "medians" likewise differ from their two-observation
averages (`analysis/paper/sweeps/tvb19_tier_a/results_by_symbol.jsonl:2371`). Across the committed
file, all 398 two-trade rows exhibit this problem; the largest trade-median error is 9.7739pp.

Separately, `_rollup` emits counts, sums, drawdown, MAE average, give-back average/p90, and weekly
totals, but no roster trade median, roster win rate, MFE summary, or give-back median
(`analysis/paper/sweep_tier_a.py:245`, `analysis/paper/sweep_tier_a.py:279`). Because per-cell event
logs are deliberately absent, the omitted roster medians cannot be reconstructed exactly from the
committed aggregates. The tests assert grid size, determinism, accounting, and DRAM exclusion, but
not median semantics or the preregistered rollup schema (`tests/test_sweep_tier_a.py:43`).

Impact: combined P/L, drawdown, weekly counts, and cell ordering are not invalidated, but the stored
distribution metrics are not the declared metrics. Use `statistics.median`, carry raw trade/episode
values into `_rollup`, assert the complete schema (including even-sized samples), and regenerate the
two JSONL artifacts plus any narrative medians derived with `_pct`.

#### 2. MEDIUM -- The committed SKHX/SKHYNIX harvest cannot be regenerated by the documented default runner

The script comments record that roster `SKHX` maps to TV coin `SKHYNIX`, yet the default coin list
still contains `SKHX` (`scripts/tvb19_harvest.mjs:12`, `scripts/tvb19_harvest.mjs:15`). It constructs
the TV identifier directly from that coin and skips it when the identifier does not load
(`scripts/tvb19_harvest.mjs:103`). The committed inventory instead contains three `SKHYNIX` datasets
(`analysis/reference/tv_deep/tvb19_harvest_summary.json:216`), consistent with the README's resolved
mapping (`analysis/reference/tv_deep/README.md:28`).

The environment override permits a partial `SKHYNIX` rerun, but every invocation rewrites the whole
summary from only that invocation's in-memory rows (`scripts/tvb19_harvest.mjs:140`). Thus the default
run cannot produce the committed 33 files, while the documented partial rerun would replace the
33-row summary with three rows unless someone performs an unrecorded manual merge. The repository
also preserves only prose for the 9,183/9,187 identity comparison, not an executable comparator or
comparison artifact.

Impact: the identity claim itself checks out -- an independent timestamp intersection over the
committed TV and HL 5m files reproduced 9,187 overlaps and 9,183 float-exact closes, and the listing
date agrees -- but its generating procedure is not reproducible. Replace the string list with an
explicit `{roster_symbol, tv_coin, tv_symbol}` mapping, generate the complete summary in one run,
fail unless all 33 datasets succeed, and commit a small identity verifier that records overlap and
mismatch counts without embedding price rows.

#### 3. MEDIUM -- "Loaded to the data floor" is fail-open, the claimed uniform 5m floor contradicts the inventory, and every dump retains a forming tail bar

`loadHistory` issues an asynchronous request, waits 700ms, and declares the floor after the first
unchanged size sample (`scripts/tvb19_harvest.mjs:58`). It can also return `err` or `capped`, but the
caller ignores those states and writes the dump anyway (`scripts/tvb19_harvest.mjs:116`). That does
not establish that TradingView reached its data floor.

The committed inventory directly contradicts the stronger prose claim that every 5m dataset has an
identical 2026-05-25 boundary (`analysis/reference/tv_deep/README.md:18`,
`docs/experiments/tvb19_overnight_report.md:145`). For example, MRVL starts 2026-05-25
(`analysis/reference/tv_deep/tvb19_harvest_summary.json:20`), AMZN starts 2026-05-18
(`analysis/reference/tv_deep/tvb19_harvest_summary.json:62`), SKHY starts 2026-07-09
(`analysis/reference/tv_deep/tvb19_harvest_summary.json:146`), and NBIS starts 2026-06-09
(`analysis/reference/tv_deep/tvb19_harvest_summary.json:167`). A common May-25 bound for symbols
already listed may still be useful, but "boundary identical across symbols" and "for every symbol"
are false as written. The same claim is repeated in commit `2fa892a`'s message.

The dump loop also serializes the active last bar without a closure flag (`scripts/tvb19_harvest.mjs:73`,
`scripts/tvb19_harvest.mjs:128`). Comparing each file's recorded harvest time with its last bar start
plus interval shows that all 33 committed files end on a still-forming bar; for one concrete artifact,
both timestamps and the interval are present on `analysis/reference/tv_deep/tvb19_tv_xyz_SKHYNIX_5m.json:1`.
The README does not declare that tail convention.

Impact: this does not contaminate the TVB-19 sweep or census, which use the separate committed HL
archive and an earlier closed window. It does make the new permanent TV reference unsafe to consume
blindly in a later replay. Wait for a reliable loading-complete state or several stable polls, fail
closed on error/cap, record the termination reason and rounds per dataset, and drop or explicitly mark
the forming tail. Correct the README/report floor language from the actual inventory.

#### 4. LOW -- The report overstates grid invariance and contains two exact rank-description errors

The qualitative reads are mostly sound, but several strong summaries do not match the rollups:

- The report says all shape knobs move marginal medians by only 2-7pp and calls the mechanism
  knob-robust (`docs/experiments/tvb19_overnight_report.md:114`). Independent aggregation of the
  committed rollups gives a 14.4853pp full span for `min_sep` (75.5083 to 89.9936pp). That remains
  smaller than the arm/backstop effects, but it is not a 2-7pp axis.
- The report says worst episode MAE sits at 37-40% and that all 864 cells establish
  config-invariance (`docs/experiments/tvb19_overnight_report.md:121`). The committed extrema are
  29.7831% and 39.6253% (`analysis/paper/sweeps/tvb19_tier_a/results_rollup.jsonl:217`,
  `analysis/paper/sweeps/tvb19_tier_a/results_rollup.jsonl:39`); 372 of 864 cells are below 37%.
  The defensible conclusion is that every cell still leaves a severe roughly 30-40% tail, not that
  the depth is invariant.
- One of the five best cells is 30m-armed, not 1H-armed
  (`docs/experiments/tvb19_overnight_report.md:70`,
  `analysis/paper/sweeps/tvb19_tier_a/results_rollup.jsonl:480`), and the second-worst cell is 15m
  with flip off, so the worst five are not all 5m with both backstops on
  (`docs/experiments/tvb19_overnight_report.md:97`,
  `analysis/paper/sweeps/tvb19_tier_a/results_rollup.jsonl:386`).

Impact: the main no-backstop warning survives -- the top 54 cells all have both backstops off -- as
does the monotone arm-timeframe marginal. Correct the exact prose and keep "structural" scoped to a
hypothesis from this one in-sample window rather than a demonstrated generalization.
